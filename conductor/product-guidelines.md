# Product Guidelines

## Prose Style and Tone
- **Technical and Precise:** All documentation, comments, and internal communications should prioritize clarity and objectivity. Descriptions of game mechanics, ghost AI, and architectural decisions must be precise to ensure the "pixel-perfect" goal is met and maintained.

## Visual Identity and Design
- **Technical Specification:** Visual design will be guided by technical specifications derived from the original arcade game. This includes:
    - **Color Palette:** Exact hex codes corresponding to the original cabinet's colors.
    - **Sprite Dimensions:** All sprites (Pac-Man, ghosts, fruit, maze elements) must adhere to their original pixel dimensions.
    - **Animation Timings:** Animation frame counts and durations must match the original 1980 arcade logic.
